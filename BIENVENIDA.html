The team
app

Everything in one place: training times, fixtures, your stats, your gym programmes and the videos. Install it once, then open it like any other app.

How to log in
Username
Your email address
Password
Your date of birth — 6 digits, no dots or slashes
Born 01.01.1990 → password 010190
What you'll find
📊
Your profile
How you're doing in serve, reception, attack and block. Match by match, or across the season.
💪
Strength
Your programme for the day with your weights. You enter them yourself and they stay saved. In the gym, the same programme runs on the big screen.
🌡️
Wellness
How you're feeling: sleep, fatigue, soreness. It takes 30 seconds and it helps us a lot when planning the session.
📅
Calendar
Training times and fixtures, always up to date. If something changes, it updates by itself.
🎬
Video
Clips of your actions, filtered however you want. To watch what we talked about in training, in your own time.
🔔
Turn on notifications. Changes to training times and matches come through the app. If you don't turn them on, you'll miss them. The PDF explains how.
In 4 steps
  1. Open volley-nafels.vercel.app on your phone (on iPhone it has to be Safari).
  2. Add it to your home screen — the PDF shows how, with screenshots.
  3. Log in with your email and date of birth.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications and you're done.

The app is in English, German and Spanish. Switch it any time with the buttons at the top right.

If you can't log in or something isn't working, message the coaching staff.
