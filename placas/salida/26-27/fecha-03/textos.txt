The setter in side-out · Gabriel Chancy

How he distributes the ball in each rotation on perfect or positive reception, that is, with every option open.

Side-out on # or + reception only: this is what he picks when he can pick. Out-of-system side-out and transition follow a different logic, and averaging them erases the pattern. Rotations are named after the setter position.

6 · The most efficient attacker
----------------------------------------------------------

The most efficient attacker · Karim Zerika

Where he attacks when he hurts, and the gap between what he returns in side-out and in transition.

Efficiency = (kill - error - blocked) / total, the standard international formula. The map keeps only the attacks that win the rally or leave the opponent with no counter-attack.

7 · The block of the round
----------------------------------------------------------

The block of the round · James Norris

The 9 best by useful block: kill block (#) plus control block (+), the one that keeps the ball playable for his own team.

The kill block is only part of it. A block that slows the ball down and leaves it defendable wins the rally just the same, which is why the table is ranked by # plus +, not by kill blocks alone.

8 · The best defender of the round
----------------------------------------------------------

The best defender of the round · Gabriel Chancy

The zones he digs from when the ball stays playable. Below, the full scale of his defence.

It is measured by the ball that stays playable, not by the one that merely gets up: a dig that saves the ball and sends it anywhere is no use. That is why it counts # plus +.

9 · Team of the round
----------------------------------------------------------

Team of the round

Outsides by attack and reception; middles by block and attack; opposite by attack; libero by reception. No position is picked on a single number.

The outside weighs 60% attack and 40% reception; the middle, half block and half attack. Every component is measured against the league average. The setter is the only one with no statistic of his own: it goes to the best attacking team's.
